About Us

M/s JEETTECNIKA is a partnership firm established on 24 November 2020, registered under the Indian Partnership Act, 1932. Headquartered at Saran, Bihar, near Rail Wheel Plant (RWP), Bela.

We deliver reliable, compliant, and innovative engineering solutions across Electrical, Mechanical, Civil Engineering, and Machining domains, integrating traditional expertise with modern and sustainable technologies.
