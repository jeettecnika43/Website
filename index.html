Annual Maintenance Contracts (AMC)

We provide comprehensive Annual Maintenance Contracts (AMC) designed to ensure optimal performance and longevity of your electrical and mechanical systems. Our AMC services are tailored to industrial and commercial clients, focusing on preventive maintenance, rapid breakdown response, and systematic equipment health monitoring.

  • Preventive maintenance scheduling and execution
  • 24/7 emergency breakdown support
  • Comprehensive equipment health monitoring
  • Spare parts management and inventory control
  • Performance optimization and energy audits
  • Compliance documentation and reporting
  • Technical training for maintenance staff
  • Annual maintenance reports and recommendations
